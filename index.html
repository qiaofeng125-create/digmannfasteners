Before You Ask

Frequently Asked Questions

What standards do your fasteners follow?
DIN, ISO, EN, ANSI/ASME, GB and JIS — e.g. DIN 933/931 hex bolts, DIN 975/976 threaded rod, DIN 125 washers, DIN 6797 tooth lock washers, ASTM A307 carriage bolts. Non-standard parts are made to your drawing.
What grades and materials do you supply?
Carbon steel 4.8, 5.8, 6.8, 8.8, 10.9, 12.9; stainless A2 (304) and A4 (316); brass and copper as required. Finishes: zinc plated, hot-dip galvanized, phosphate, Dacromet, plain.
What is your minimum order quantity?
Standard items from 500 pcs for trial orders; bulk container quantities for regular SKUs. Mixed containers across several SKUs are welcome.
Can you manufacture to our drawing?
Yes. Send a drawing or a clear sample photo with quantity, grade and finish. We reply within 24 hours with a quotation; typical sampling in 3–7 days.
How do you control quality?
Incoming material check, in-process checks and final AQL 2.5 inspection. Hardness, tensile, salt-spray and dimensional reports per batch, with batch traceability on every carton.
How are fasteners packed for export?
Inner boxes or bags with your labels, outer heat-treated (fumigation-free) plywood export crates, thread protection caps on studs, palletized and lashed for container transit.
Which Incoterms do you offer?
FOB Shanghai, CIF main ports, and DDP door-to-door by sea or air. Samples go by DHL / FedEx / UPS.
Are free samples available?
Yes for standard items — you only cover the courier cost. Custom samples are quoted per drawing.

Tell Us What You Need Quoted
